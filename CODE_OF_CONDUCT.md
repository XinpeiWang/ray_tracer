# Code of conduct

This is a small technical project and we want it to be a place where people can ask questions,
report bugs and disagree about numbers without it getting personal.

* Be respectful. Argue with the code, the measurement or the idea, not with the person.
* Welcome beginners. Graphics programming is hard, and "why is this black?" is a good question.
* No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing other people's private information.
* Assume good faith, and say what you could not check instead of guessing.
* Credit other people's work, and keep the licence terms of anything you reuse
  (see [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)).

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may
ban people who keep doing so. To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment or
contact the maintainer through the profile linked on the repository.
